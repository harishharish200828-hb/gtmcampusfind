How it works

1. Submit a lost or found item with its details.

2. Add a photo to help others identify the item.

3. Search and filter the listings to find a match.

4. Open the details and send a contact/request message.

5. Mark the listing as resolved when the case is complete.

About CampusFind

CampusFind is a campus Lost & Found portal designed to make reporting, discovering and returning belongings easier. It supports item details, image preview, search, category and status filters, detailed listings, contact/request messages, and active or resolved records.

Student Project: HARISH S · BCA · Government Thirumagal Mills College
